Support

Found a bug, have a question or an idea? Write to mle.engineers.sl+scrolldojo@gmail.com — I usually reply within a few days. Mentioning your iPhone model and iOS version helps.

Frequently asked questions

Why does Scroll Dojo need Screen Time access?

It is the only way iOS lets an app measure time spent in other apps and show a gate over them. The data never leaves your iPhone — see the privacy policy.

Usage or the gate doesn't seem to update.

Screen Time counts usage only from the moment monitoring starts, so minutes spent before setting up the app are not counted. Make sure Screen Time is turned on in Settings and that Scroll Dojo still has access (Settings → Screen Time).

How do I reset or delete my data?

All data is on your device. Deleting the app removes it completely.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 26 or later.
